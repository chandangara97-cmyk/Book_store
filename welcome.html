Questions parents ask

How do I know a book suits my child?

Each book shows an age range. Use the slider above, and remember that children learn at their own pace, so a book just below or above their age can also work well.

What is the difference between printed and digital?

The content is the same. A printed copy is delivered to you. A digital copy is read on a screen and costs less.

Which payment methods can I use?

Razorpay supports UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and popular wallets.

Can I ask for a book on a topic you do not have yet?

Yes. Send us a message with the age and topic, and we will tell you what can be arranged.

Need help choosing?

Tell us your child's age and interests, and we will suggest the right book.
